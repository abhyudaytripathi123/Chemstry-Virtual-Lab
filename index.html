Lab Safety Guidelines & Protocol

Personal Protective Equipment Always wear safety goggles, lab aprons or coats, and protective gloves during experiments. Closed shoes are mandatory.
  • Do not eat, drink, or chew gum inside the laboratory.
  • Read and check chemical labels carefully before use. Handle acids and glassware with extreme care.
  • In case of eye contact with chemicals, wash immediately with copious water for at least 10 minutes.
  • Report all accidents and broken glassware immediately to the faculty supervisor.
